# Hanyang Daily Brief

Use the Hanyang school connection inside the unified Study plugin. Verify it with get_study_profile; never substitute the Berkeley connection. This does not require switching the user's ChatGPT account. Preserve the scheduled task's existing timing and use Asia/Seoul for coursework dates.

Help the student quickly understand how to attend class, what to do and what to prepare. Read [Canvas](../canvas/SKILL.md) for source interpretation, access, file handling and continuation rules. Use the requested date, or today, in Asia/Seoul.

Start with the valid imported timetable. Check all current-term courses for requirements, including courses without a meeting today. Combine relevant announcements, LearningX boards and weekly modules, Canvas Inbox, assignments and submissions, quizzes, calendar events and course materials. Inspect relevant bodies, teacher replies and linked pages or attachments when they contain requirements; titles alone are insufficient. Do not routinely summarize all lectures or download the whole course library.

Check date-specific changes for today's meetings, and retain older notices and standing rules that still apply. Do not restrict discovery to unread items or the past day. Without a reliable previous-run record, reread relevant sources instead of claiming nothing changed. Include undated obligations, video attendance and preparation outside the assignment list. Use school email only when requested and connected; disclose missing requested coverage without making it a prerequisite for the Hanyang check.

Recheck current completion before carrying any task forward; use the assignment's submission detail for submission status.

Build assignment coverage from unfiltered course assignment pages with own submissions, continuing every nextCursor for all current-term courses. Follow Canvas's status rules for missing, rework and unknown results; Planner and date buckets are discovery aids and cannot replace this inventory. Keep a per-course/source coverage record, including remaining pages and errors, before deciding that nothing is due. This inventory covers assignments only: the other requirement sources above still need their own checks.

Write a compact Chinese reminder with the date and Korean-time context, using two main sections:

- **今天的课**: Include every meeting today with its effective time, location or online arrangement. Keep that meeting's textbooks, things to bring and explicit pre-class reading beside it; if a required book is missing, include the purchase action there. Put urgent changes or conflicts first within the affected course. Do not invent lesson topics.
- **待完成事项**: Combine assignments, videos, questionnaires, replies, group-joining and other independently completable tasks. Start each item with a concrete action such as 交作业, 完成视频 or 发送MBTI, followed by the course, known deadline and useful link. Keep assignments visible here even when their course meets today. Distinguish an opening time from the deadline or lock time. Order unresolved overdue work first, then today's deadlines, then later deadlines; label unknown completion and omit completed actions. Include today's deadlines and unresolved overdue work in full. Normally prioritize the next three days, but retain known unfinished tasks the user is tracking and flag work or purchases earlier when preparation is needed. A previous reminder is not a reason to hide an approaching deadline.

List preparation for a future class here when it needs advance attention; on the class day, place its materials and reading under that course instead. Show each action once. Put an important future timetable change in a short line after the two sections rather than turning it into a task. When a section has nothing to report, use one short factual line such as 今日无课 or 暂无已确认需完成的事项 only when the check supports it. Weekends still need checks for explicit classes, exams and tasks.

Merge duplicate facts across sources without losing additional requirements. Keep the normal timetable for comparison, but show only the effective arrangement once resolved. Omit tool names, Skill-loading confirmations, retrieval reasoning and completed-work explanations from ordinary reminders; report them only when explicitly requested for a test or needed to explain an actionable unresolved issue. Put relevant read-only material links beside their course or task; never assume a prior download is accessible in this run. Do not upload files to Canvas or attach files to Inbox messages. Omit unchanged background and additional empty sections. Aim for a screenful, but never drop necessary actions to meet a word or item limit.

Before finishing, check that the required courses and sources were covered. Briefly state gaps or conflicts and their practical impact; an incomplete check cannot support an all-clear. Follow the existing quiet-notification preference only when the check succeeded and there is nothing relevant. This workflow reads and reports; it does not create schedules, send messages or change school records.
